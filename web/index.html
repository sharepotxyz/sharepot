Bet with your stock tokens. Win more shares.

Daily pools on where US stocks close. Stake the tokenized stock you already hold — xStocks, Backpack or Ondo — and winners are paid in that same token. Nothing gets sold.

  1. 1Pick a rangeBig drop, small drop, small gain or big gain — for today's close vs yesterday's.
  2. 2Stake sharesBets stop at the opening bell. A drop bet hedges shares you keep; a gain bet adds upside.
  3. 3Get paid in sharesAfter the official close, winners split the losing pools. Automatic, verifiable on-chain.

SharePot is a parimutuel pool, not an order book: payouts depend on the final pool sizes. Fee 3% of winnings (2% early), never on your stake. Results are disputable for one hour, then paid out automatically.

Issuer controls. Stock tokens are issued by xStocks (Backed), Backpack Securities and Ondo Global Markets, who can pause transfers of a token; xStocks and Backpack tokens also let the issuer move tokens in any account, these pools included. Tokenized stocks are not offered to US persons.
